Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 June 2026.

Donguri House LLC ("dongurihouse," "we," "us") develops games and operates this website (dongurihouse.net). This policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and our games.

The short version: we do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. Our games run on your device, purchases are handled by Apple, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

This website

This site is static. It has no accounts, sets no advertising cookies, and does not track you across other sites. If we enable privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics), they record only aggregate, non-identifying metrics — never personal data.

Our games

We do not collect personal information through our games. Your game progress and settings are stored on your device. On the App Store, our games carry the "Data Not Collected" privacy label.

  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment information — we never receive your card or payment details, only Apple's confirmation that a purchase completed so we can unlock the content. Apple's handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • No accounts. You don't need to create an account or sign in to play.
  • No tracking SDKs. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing. We do not sell or share your information — there is no personal data for us to sell.

Children

Our games are suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Your choices

Because we don't collect personal data, there is nothing on our side for us to access, correct, or delete. Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app. For purchase or subscription questions, manage them in your Apple account settings or contact Apple.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our games evolve. If we ever add a feature that collects or transmits data — for example optional rewarded ads, online or social features (such as Game Center or leaderboards), or server-synced content — we will update this page and our App Store privacy details before that feature ships, and revise the "last updated" date above.
